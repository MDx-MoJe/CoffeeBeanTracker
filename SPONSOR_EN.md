# Support the Developer

[简体中文](SPONSOR.md) | English

BeanBag (CoffeeBeanTracker) is a completely free, open-source, ad-free, fully offline coffee inventory app. It never collects your data and never paywalls any feature.

If it helps you keep your beans organized, here are ways to support the development:

## Afdian (爱发电)

**[Support on Afdian →](https://afdian.com/a/RoastCurve)**

Afdian is a creator sponsorship platform where you can make a one-time donation or a monthly pledge. (BeanBag and RoastCurve share the same sponsor channel.)

## Other Ways

- **GitHub**: starring the repo ⭐ and recommending it to coffee friends is great support
- **Code / Issues**: open an Issue or Pull Request — see [CONTRIBUTING_EN.md](CONTRIBUTING_EN.md)
- **Try the sister app [RoastCurve](https://github.com/MDx-MoJe/RoastCurve)**: if you roast your own beans, it's the perfect companion to BeanBag

---

> With or without sponsorship, BeanBag stays free, open-source, and ad-free. Thank you for your trust and company.
